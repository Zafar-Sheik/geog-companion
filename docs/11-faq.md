# FAQ & troubleshooting

### My progress disappeared!

Progress is saved in the browser on one device. Clearing browser data, using a private/incognito window or switching devices starts fresh. Use **Progress → Export** to save a file and **Import** on the new device.

### Is this the official syllabus?

GeoMzansi follows the CAPS Grade 12 Geography content and the NSC exam format, but it is an independent study aid. Always confirm the current exam guidelines and any focus areas with your teacher.

### Read-aloud doesn't work

Read-aloud uses your browser's built-in speech. Most modern browsers support it; some need a voice to be installed in the operating system's settings.

### The animations are distracting

Turn on **Calm mode** in Preferences (or the reduce-motion setting on your device).

### Why is the map of South Africa not exact?

The economic and weather maps are deliberately **schematic** – simplified outlines that show where things are, not maps for measuring. Use the official topographic maps for measurements.

### Can I use it offline?

After a page has loaded once, most of it keeps working with a weak connection, but the site is not a full offline app.
