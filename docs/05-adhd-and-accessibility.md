# ADHD & accessibility

Open **Preferences** (the accessibility icon in the top bar). Every setting is saved on the device.

## Focus helpers

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **One step at a time** (on by default) | Lessons show a single chunk with a clear Next button and a step list |
| **Focus mode** | Hides navigation and footers so only the lesson remains |
| **Calm mode** | Switches off decorative animation (also follows the operating system's *reduce motion* setting) |
| **Reading ruler** | A highlighted band follows the pointer to help keep your place |
| **Word anchors** | Bolds the first half of each word in lesson text to guide the eye |
| **Focus sprints** | A 5/10/15/25-minute timer that ends with a short movement break |

## Reading & display

- Fonts: **Inter**, **Atkinson Hyperlegible** and **OpenDyslexic**
- Four text sizes and three line-spacing options
- Light, dark and **high-contrast** themes
- **Read aloud** on every lesson step and flashcard (Web Speech API, South African English voice where available)

## Interaction

- Every activity works with a **mouse, a finger or a keyboard** – tapping instead of drag-and-drop.
- Lessons: ← → keys move between steps. Flashcards: space flips, → *knew it*, ← *not yet*.
- Visible focus rings, skip link, ARIA labels on interactive diagrams, and live regions for feedback.

## Consistent colour meaning

Each topic has one colour everywhere (see *Colours, type & design system*). Red is used **only** for mistakes and danger; pink only for memory hooks; amber for exam tips. Colour is never the only signal – icons and text always accompany it.
