# CAPS curriculum map

How GeoMzansi lessons map to the **CAPS Grade 12 Geography** content and the **NSC examination papers**.

## Exam structure

| Paper | Duration | Marks | Questions |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Paper 1** – Physical Geography | 3 hours | 150 | Q1 Climate & Weather (60) · Q2 Geomorphology (60) · Q3 Geographical Skills & Techniques (30) |
| **Paper 2** – Human Geography | 3 hours | 150 | Q1 Rural & Urban Settlement (60) · Q2 Economic Geography of SA (60) · Q3 Geographical Skills & Techniques (30) |

Question 3 in each paper (mapwork) uses a 1 : 50 000 topographic map and a 1 : 10 000 orthophoto map, and is usually split into map calculations, map interpretation (linked to the theory in that paper) and GIS. School-Based Assessment counts 25% of the final mark.

> Mark allocations follow the DBE Grade 12 Geography examination guidelines. Guidelines are updated from time to time – confirm the current year's details with your teacher.

## Paper 1 – Climate & Weather

| Lesson | CAPS content |
|---|---|
| Mid-latitude cyclones | Formation on the polar front, life cycle, cold/warm/occluded fronts, cross-sections, weather changes, impact on SA |
| Tropical cyclones | Conditions, location, stages, structure (eye, eye wall, rain bands), dangerous quadrant, impacts, management |
| Subtropical anticyclones & SA weather | South Atlantic, South Indian and Kalahari highs; seasonal shifts; berg winds; coastal lows; moisture front and line thunderstorms |
| Reading synoptic weather maps | Isobars, pressure gradient, station models, identifying season |
| Valley climates | Anabatic/katabatic winds, inversions, frost pockets, thermal belts, aspect, human impact |
| Urban climates | Urban heat island, pollution dome, urban vs rural climate, sustainable strategies |

## Paper 1 – Geomorphology

| Lesson | CAPS content |
|---|---|
| Drainage basins | Basin terminology, river types (permanent, periodic, episodic, exotic), drainage density, run-off & infiltration |
| Drainage patterns & stream order | Eight drainage patterns and rock structure; Strahler stream ordering |
| Fluvial processes & landforms | Erosion, transport, deposition; upper, middle and lower course landforms; meanders, oxbow lakes, braided streams, levées, deltas |
| River profiles, grading & rejuvenation | Longitudinal/transverse profiles, graded streams, base level, rejuvenation landforms |
| River capture, superimposed & antecedent drainage | Captor, captured, misfit stream, elbow of capture, wind gap; drainage evolution |
| Catchment & river management | Human impacts, floods, wetlands, SA management strategies |

## Paper 2 – Settlement

| Lesson | CAPS content |
|---|---|
| Rural settlements | Site & situation, pattern, shape, function, rural–urban migration, rural depopulation, land reform, food security |
| Urbanisation, hierarchies & functions | Urbanisation, urban hierarchy, central place theory (range, threshold, sphere of influence), urban functions |
| Urban structure & land-use models | Land-use zones, CBD, decentralisation, street patterns, Burgess, Hoyt, Harris & Ullman, apartheid & post-apartheid city |
| Urban settlement issues | Informal settlements, congestion, blight, inner-city decay, gentrification, sprawl, sustainable cities |

## Paper 2 – Economic Geography of SA

| Lesson | CAPS content |
|---|---|
| Structure of SA's economy | Economic sectors, GDP/GNP, contribution to GDP and employment, trade |
| Agriculture & food security | Importance, favourable/limiting factors, food security and strategies |
| Mining | Significance, factors, challenges, environmental impact |
| Secondary sector, industrial regions & SDIs | Location factors, four core industrial regions, decentralisation, SDIs and SEZs (IDZs) |
| The informal sector | Characteristics, growth, challenges, contribution, support |

## Geographical Skills & Techniques (both papers)

| Lesson | CAPS content |
|---|---|
| Map skills & calculations | Scale, distance, area, gradient, bearings, magnetic declination, vertical exaggeration, position |
| Map & photo interpretation | Topographic and orthophoto maps, landforms from contours, cross-sections, intervisibility, applying theory to maps |
| GIS | Spatial/attribute data, raster/vector, resolution, layers, buffering, querying, overlay, remote sensing |
