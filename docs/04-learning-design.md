# Learning design & study tools

GeoMzansi is built on a small number of well-researched learning principles.

## Chunking

Every lesson is split into steps of **3–8 minutes**, shown one at a time with a clear *Next* button. Learners can stop after any step and the app resumes where they left off.

## Dual coding

Ideas are presented in **words and pictures together**. Processes that are hard to imagine – a front passing a weather station, a river capturing another, a knickpoint retreating – are shown as interactive diagrams.

## Active learning

Each lesson includes at least one thing to *do*:

- **Sorters** – e.g. push vs pull factors, economic sectors, site vs situation
- **Put it in order** – e.g. stages of a mid-latitude cyclone, formation of an oxbow lake
- **Match column A to column B** – mirrors Question 1 style items in the NSC papers
- **Simulators** – urban heat island sliders, valley day/night, rejuvenation slider, GIS buffering

## Retrieval practice and spaced repetition

- **Quick quizzes** at the end of every lesson give instant feedback with an explanation.
- **Flashcards** use the Leitner system: a card you know moves up a box (seen again in 1, 2, 4, 8, then 16 days); a card you miss goes back to box 1.
- **Quiz Arena** mixes topics (interleaving), which improves long-term retention and exam readiness.

## Exam literacy

The **Exam Room** teaches the *language of the exam*: action verbs, cognitive levels, one fact per mark, paragraph questions and calculation layout. Learners write their answer **first**, then self-mark against a memo – the same way examiners mark.

## Learning styles

Lessons are tagged *visual*, *auditory*, *read/write* and *hands-on* so teachers can see the mix. Every chunk can be **read aloud**, and every diagram has text explanations.

## Motivation

XP, levels, streaks and badges reward **effort and consistency** rather than only correct answers. Wrong answers are framed as "Not yet".
