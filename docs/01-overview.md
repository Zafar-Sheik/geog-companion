# Overview

**GeoMzansi** is an interactive Grade 12 Geography companion built for the learners of **Northwood Boys' High School** in Durban. It follows the **CAPS Grade 12 Geography** curriculum and the **NSC examination format** (Paper 1 and Paper 2) written by KwaZulu-Natal public schools.

It is a sister app to *CodeMzansi*, the school's IT learning platform, and uses the same learning design, accessibility features and look-and-feel.

## What's inside

| Area | What learners do |
|---|---|
| **Learn** | 24 bite-sized lessons across the five examinable topics, each split into 3–8 minute steps |
| **Toolkit** | 20 interactive tools – cyclone simulators, river profiles, urban models, map calculators, cross-sections, a GIS lab |
| **Flashcards** | Every key concept in a spaced-repetition (Leitner) deck, plus a searchable glossary |
| **Quiz Arena** | Mixed-topic multiple-choice rounds, relaxed or timed |
| **Exam Room** | NSC-style data-response case studies with sources, figures and marking memos for self-marking |
| **Games** | Concept Pairs, True/False Blitz, Odd One Out and Definition Hangman |
| **Progress** | XP, levels, streaks, badges, flashcard mastery and exam averages |

## The five topics

1. **Climate & Weather** (Paper 1) – mid-latitude and tropical cyclones, subtropical anticyclones and SA weather, synoptic maps, valley and urban climates
2. **Geomorphology** (Paper 1) – drainage basins, drainage patterns and stream order, fluvial landforms, profiles and rejuvenation, river capture, catchment management
3. **Settlement** (Paper 2) – rural settlement, urbanisation and hierarchies, urban structure and models, urban issues
4. **Economic Geography of SA** (Paper 2) – structure of the economy, agriculture and food security, mining, industrial regions and SDIs/SEZs, the informal sector
5. **Mapwork & GIS** (Section B of both papers) – calculations, map and photo interpretation, GIS

## Principles

- **Learning by doing** – every concept comes with something to click, drag, sort, predict or calculate.
- **Retrieval practice** – quizzes, flashcards, exam questions and games all make learners *recall* rather than re-read.
- **Every brain is welcome** – ADHD and dyslexia supports are built in, not bolted on.
- **Private by design** – no accounts and no tracking; progress lives only on the learner's device.

> GeoMzansi is an independent study aid. It does not replace the teacher, the textbook or the official DBE examination guidelines – always check exam details with your teacher.
