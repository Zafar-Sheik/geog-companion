# Teacher quick-start

Ten minutes to get your class going.

## 1. Share the link

Learners open the site on any phone, tablet or computer. There is nothing to install and no sign-up. Progress is saved in the browser on that device.

## 2. Pick a lesson to match your teaching

Use **Learn → Paper 1 / Paper 2** or filter by topic. Lessons follow the order of the CAPS Annual Teaching Plan within each topic. Each lesson lists its **objectives** and **CAPS reference** at the top.

## 3. Use it in class

- **Project a tool** from the **Toolkit** while you teach – e.g. step through the *mid-latitude cyclone life cycle*, toggle *day/night in a valley*, or drop the sea level in *river profiles & rejuvenation*.
- **Think–pair–share:** let pairs do a *Put it in order* or *Match* activity, then discuss.
- **Exit ticket:** finish with the lesson's *Check yourself* quiz.

## 4. Set homework

- **Flashcards → Due today** for 5–10 minutes a day (spaced repetition works best little and often).
- One **Exam Room** case study per week. Learners write their answers *before* opening the memo, then tick the points they made.
- A **Quiz Arena** round on mixed topics before a test.

## 5. Check progress

Learners can show you their **Progress** page (XP, streak, badges, lessons completed, exam average). They can **export** their progress to a file if they change device.

## Tips

- Encourage the **Preferences** panel (accessibility icon) – fonts, text size, calm mode, reading ruler and read-aloud help many learners.
- The **focus timer** (top bar) runs short sprints with movement breaks – useful for study periods.
- Map calculations in the **Toolkit** have a *Practice* mode that generates unlimited new questions with full memo-style working.
